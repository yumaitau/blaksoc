# Discovery session guide

> **DRAFT.** Not reviewed by the advisory group or any participant. Change the topics, wording and format
> with the group before the first session. These are questions, not assumptions about how any
> organisation works.

## Before each session
- Confirm consent for this session (see [session-consent.md](session-consent.md)) and whether notes or
  recordings are allowed.
- Agree what must not be written down.
- Confirm payment for participants' time.

## Topics from issue #1

1. **How organisations hold cultural knowledge and sensitive records.** What kinds of records exist (for
   example heritage or native title material, Sorry Business, gender-restricted material)? Where are they
   kept today? What would harm look like if they were exposed?
2. **Who is authorised to see what.** Who decides access today? Should a security provider's analysts ever
   see certain records? What should happen when an incident needs that access?
3. **Data leaving the organisation.** Is sharing anonymous threat information with other organisations
   acceptable, and on whose authority? Should AI ever process this data, and for what?
4. **Communicating incidents.** How should a security incident be explained to a board, to community and to
   affected people? Who should hear first? What makes a notice respectful or harmful?
5. **Governance of the product.** Who in an organisation should approve changes to data rules? How many
   people? What should they be told, and how?

## After each session
- De-identify findings within an agreed time.
- Read back key points to participants before recording any decision.
- Add agreed outcomes to the [decision log](decision-log.md).
